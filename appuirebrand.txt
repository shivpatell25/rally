RALLY UI REBRAND PLAN

Overall direction

Create a premium sports interface combining:

- Apple TV-style cinematic layouts and spacing
- ESPN-style editorial presentation
- Rally’s approved gradient identity and dark navy canvas
- Oversized team monograms and logo-driven identity
- A clear, editorial sports-broadcast hierarchy

Approved brand kit

The approved Rally production kit is included in the project at:

- `Rally_Brand_Kit/`

Use the kit’s canonical assets rather than recreating or recoloring the brand:

- `01_Master/rally_primary_app_icon_4096.png`
- `01_Master/rally_primary_wordmark_4096.png`
- `01_Master/rally_primary_hero_centered_4096.png`
- `04_Lockups/` for horizontal and stacked lockups
- `08_Vector_SVG/` for scalable logo and mark variants
- `09_Brand_Tokens/brand-colors.json` and `rally-tokens.css` as the color-token source of truth

Primary tagline: “Sports kept simple.”

Use the full-color gradient logo on deep/dark surfaces, white logo variants over busy imagery, and black/deep-navy variants only on light surfaces. Transparent logo assets must remain transparent.

Functionality, navigation behavior, game cards, players, streams, and data remain unchanged.

The rebrand must not reduce performance. It should improve performance wherever possible, especially scrolling, focus movement, screen transitions, image loading, and startup on Chromecast with Google TV.

Brand palette

- Rally Lime — #EAFB78
- Rally Mint — #B8F3C7
- Rally Cyan — #6FCFF6
- Deep Navy — #05080F
- Slate — #0F1724
- Graphite — #202834
- Off-White — #F5F7FA

Use the linear Rally gradient from lime through mint and aqua to cyan for logos, selected states, focus accents, and high-value moments. The interface should remain primarily deep navy, slate, and graphite, with lime and cyan used selectively rather than as large flat fills.

Logo system

- Full-color Rally wordmark: splash screen, home header, and major branded areas on deep navy.
- White Rally wordmark: player, settings, overlays, and dark surfaces.
- Rally monogram: app icon, compact navigation, and loading states.
- Transparent gradient, lime, or white marks: team, league, player, and editorial surfaces.
- Lime/white wordmarks on deep navy: premium launch and promotional moments.

Monogram backdrop language

- Replace the current sports backdrops with newly generated ESPN-style sports backdrops built around Rally monograms.
- Generate a distinct editorial backdrop treatment for each major sport and league while keeping the Rally visual system consistent.
- Use these newly generated monogram sports backdrops inside the dedicated hero box, vertical game cards, team pages, league pages, and player/game-view surfaces where appropriate.
- Do not reuse the current generic sports backdrops as the final rebrand artwork.
- Use oversized, cropped, low-opacity monograms.
- Favor white, gray, or muted logo treatments.
- Use dark gradients over photographs and patterns.
- Reserve strong team colors for small accents and selected content.

Monograms create identity; color communicates state.

Home screen

1. Rally-branded header and navigation
2. A dedicated, large ESPN-style featured-game hero box
3. My Teams
4. Up Next
5. Live Now
6. NFL and RedZone
7. Existing league shelves with vertical game cards

Hero box requirement

The hero must be its own large contained box/card with a clear boundary from the page background, similar to ESPN’s editorial feature treatment. It must not be blended into, or rendered as, one uninterrupted full-screen background.

- Use a large landscape container with subtly rounded, boxy corners.
- Keep the Rally hero artwork, matchup backdrop, or game photography inside the box.
- Apply an internal gradient for text legibility, not a full-page gradient that erases the box boundary.
- Place matchup title, league, time/status, and primary action inside the hero box.
- Preserve enough surrounding deep-navy space that the hero reads as a featured content module.
- Use the approved Rally hero artwork primarily for branded, empty, splash, or promotional hero states; do not place large text directly over the centered Rally mark.

Game cards

Preserve the vertical cards while refining:

- Dark surfaces
- Larger team logos
- Clear matchup and score hierarchy
- Consistent sizing and spacing
- White primary text and gray metadata
- Lime/cyan focus border or restrained glow
- Graphite, slate, and deep-navy surfaces
- Boxy cards with subtly rounded corners, not pill-shaped cards
- Use consistent small-to-medium corner radii across cards and shelves

Buttons and controls

- Buttons must use boxy, subtly rounded corners rather than pill-shaped capsules.
- Reserve pill shapes for compact status badges, live indicators, and metadata chips only.
- Use clear rectangular focus states with a Rally lime or cyan edge/accent.
- Keep button height, padding, and focus treatment consistent across Home, Player, Settings, and source pickers.
- Avoid excessive glassmorphism, giant radii, or floating bubbles that weaken the ESPN-style editorial structure.

Game detail screen

Keep the existing layout:

- Same-aspect-ratio player on the left
- Game information and analytics on the right
- Subtle matchup monograms in the background
- Minimal controls until interaction
- Lime focus states for controls and selections
- Boxy, subtly rounded information and control panels consistent with the Rally system

Team and league pages

- Monogram-led hero sections
- Dark editorial shelves
- Existing game cards
- Consistent tabs and headings
- Team colors used only as accents
- Team pages shown only for favorited teams

Performance requirement

The UI work must improve or maintain current performance:

- Avoid unnecessary recomposition
- Reuse existing image and logo-loading logic
- Cache and resize backdrop assets appropriately
- Avoid oversized unoptimized images
- Keep animations short and lightweight
- Preserve smooth remote focus movement
- Avoid adding expensive blur, shader, or parallax effects
- Maintain fast startup and screen transitions
- Test scrolling and navigation on Chromecast with Google TV

Final goal

Rally’s “Sports, kept simple. Rally” identity, Apple TV cinematic polish, ESPN-style editorial hero box, boxy subtly rounded controls and cards, preserved functionality and game cards, with equal or better performance than the current app.
